I have decided to edit the Snake Game template. My chosen inspiration is the magical girl anime Puella Magi Madoka Magica. I chose it, because there is a scene in it, where a snake witch eats the head off of a character called Mami.
The aesthetic changes I want to make are to change the snake sprite into the image of the witch, and the food sprite into the image of Mami. I also want to change the background to look more like the labyrinth where this scene happens. Finally, I want to add some background music, if possible. 
Moreover, I want to make the snake die when it hits a corner or eats itself. I think I'll keep the speed of the snake, because in the tv show scene, the reason why the witch was able to eat Mami was because of her faster-than-light speed which did not allow Mami to react.
