==**PROTOTYPES AS FILTERS**==
A lot of the times, when thinking of something, we add a bunch of different things to it that we believe will make sense once put together into one thing. As we test and examine the feasibility, we realize that some things work better than others.  

==**PROTOTYPES AS MANIFESTATIONS**==
A prototype is an externalization of an abstract concept, whether it be digital, physical, or conceptual. What was once just an idea in the designer's head becomes visible and palpable. Therefore, it can be a sort of manifestation. 

This makes me think of times, late at night, when I had plenty of good ideas for certain projects, but was too tired to write it down and assumed I would still remember it the next morning. Eight times out of ten, my assumptions turn out to be wrong. 

It is important to write down, draft, prototype any concept that we come up with because of this specific issue. Ideas come and go, and we are the ones who have to hold on to it, because someone else can come up with it too. 

I once heard about this theory (which I forgot the name of), where multiple people can have the EXACT same idea, but it all comes down to who does it first.
